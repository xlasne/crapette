About Crapette Pro

A classic French card game, rebuilt for your iPhone

Crapette is a two-player card game that's been quietly testing families' patience — and friendships — for generations. Crapette Pro brings it to iPhone, with a sharp AI opponent, so you can play a proper game whenever you like, without waiting for someone to sit down across the table.

How it's played

Each player starts with their own 52-card deck, split across three piles. The goal is simple to say and hard to do: be the first to empty both your Draw and Discard piles. Along the way, both players share a board of foundation piles (built up by suit, Ace to King) and open rows in the middle, so a good move for you often opens — or closes — an opportunity for your opponent.

The catch: "Crapette!"

What sets Crapette apart from most solitaire-style games is that moves have to follow a strict order of priority — and if you play out of turn or skip a move you should have made, your opponent can call "Crapette!" to challenge you. Catch them, and their move gets reversed and their turn ends early. It's the rule that gives the game its name, and its reputation: half the game is watching your own moves as closely as your opponent's.

In the app

Crapette Pro plays a full, careful AI opponent that follows the same priority rules a sharp human player would — no shortcuts, no cheating. You'll find hints when you're stuck, undo when you misclick, and a rank that climbs the fewer challenges you rack up, all the way to Ultimate Grandmaster.
