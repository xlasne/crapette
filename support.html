Support

Contact and frequently asked questions

Have a question, a problem, or a suggestion? Write to me — I read every message and reply myself, in English or French.

  • Email support

Email: xavier.crapette.pro@gmail.com

You can also write from inside the app: open Feedback from the home screen and tap Email the developer. If you need help with a problem, email is better than an App Store review: I can only reply to a review once, while by email we can go back and forth until it's solved.

To help me help you, please mention your device (iPhone or iPad model), your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot is always welcome.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

On the home screen, open the ⋯ menu, choose Purchases, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. The purchase is restored for free.

What can I play without buying anything?

Everything: every rule level, every opponent, every aid, with no time limit — on 10 trial deals that come round in a loop. The one-time purchase opens new deals, endlessly. There is no subscription.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays entirely offline. The network is only used when you buy or restore the purchase.

What data does the app collect?

None. No account, no analytics, no advertising. See the privacy policy.

I don't know the rules of Crapette. Where do I start?

The first game opens on a short tutorial. Then, in any game, tap ? : every element of the board explains itself. At the Student level, target cards and hints show you what to play — and every "Stop!" you receive shows the move that was expected and the rule behind it.

Can two people play on one device?

Yes. Choose a human opponent: the board turns toward each player, and the computer can referee the calls.

The computer called "Stop!" on me — why?

You missed a move the rules of your level required. Tap the ⓘ on the card that appears: it shows the expected move and the rule, and opens the rules tree at the right question.
