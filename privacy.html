Privacy Policy

How Crapette Pro handles your data

This policy explains what information Crapette Pro collects, why, and what control you have over it. Crapette Pro is a solitaire-style card game for iPhone — it doesn't need to know who you are to work, and it's built that way.

Information we collect

Stored only on your device

Your game progress, statistics and preferences never leave your phone. This includes:

  • Your current game (card positions and move history), so you can resume where you left off
  • Game counters, such as games played, wins and losses
  • Your settings, such as display and gameplay options

None of this is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes this data with it, and you can remove an individual saved game at any time from within the app.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

Crapette Pro uses Mixpanel, a third-party analytics service, to understand how the game is used — for example, which screens are opened, how a game unfolds, and whether a purchase was completed. This helps us fix problems and improve the game. We only work with analytics providers, like Mixpanel, that protect this data to the same standard described in this policy, and we don't allow it to be used for anything beyond providing analytics to us. You can review Mixpanel's own commitments in their Data Processing Addendum and security overview.

This analytics data is anonymous: it is tied to a randomly generated ID, not to your name, email, Apple ID, or device. We never link it to your identity, and no personal information — name, email, location, contacts, or photos — is ever collected or sent.

You can turn this off at any time from Settings → Data Privacy → Disable data logging inside the app. Once disabled, no gameplay data is sent to Mixpanel, and your choice is remembered across app launches. Analytics is also always off in development/test builds of the app.

In-app purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks the full version of Crapette Pro is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Payment details and receipts are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy — we never see or store your payment information.

What we don't collect

Crapette Pro does not collect your name, email address, account credentials, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, browsing history, or any health or financial information. We don't use advertising SDKs, and there is no crash-reporting service integrated into the app.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the app or remove a specific saved game. Anonymous analytics events sent to Mixpanel are retained under Mixpanel's own data retention policy; turning off data logging stops any further events from being sent.

Children's privacy

Crapette Pro is intended for a general audience and is not directed at or designed for children under 13 — its strategic, multi-step gameplay is best suited to older players. If a younger child plays, for example alongside a parent, we still do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, regardless of age, and the anonymous analytics described above are never linked to an individual's identity. If you're a parent and believe your child has shared information with us that they shouldn't have, contact us using the details below and we'll look into it.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can disable analytics at any time from within the app — see "Anonymous gameplay analytics" above. If you're in the EU/EEA or California and have questions about how this applies to you under GDPR or CCPA, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
