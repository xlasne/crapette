Privacy Policy

Crapette Pro collects no data at all. Here is what that means.

This policy explains what Crapette Pro does with your data. The short version: it collects nothing. Crapette Pro is a solitaire-style card game for iPhone — it doesn't need to know who you are to work, and it's built that way.

What we collect

Nothing. No account, no name, no statistics, no advertising, no third-party analytics, no crash reporting. Nothing about the way you play leaves your device.

Earlier versions of this page described anonymous gameplay statistics, sent to a third-party service and switchable off. They were removed in September 2026, and with them the third-party code that carried them. The reason is simple: the one thing those figures were meant to answer was whether the difficulty is well tuned — and with six difficulty levels already covering every taste, no figure would have made me change the game. Rather than collect data I would not act on, I stopped collecting it.

The app's App Store listing says the same thing, in Apple's own words: Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

The game remembers things, but they stay with you:

  • Your current game (card positions and move history), so you can resume where you left off
  • Game counters, such as games played, wins and losses
  • Your settings, such as display and gameplay options

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It travels only with your own iCloud backup, if you use one, under Apple's terms rather than mine. Deleting the app deletes this data with it, and you can remove an individual saved game at any time from within the app.

When the app uses the network

You never need the network to play. The app connects for one thing only: the purchase.

In-app purchases

The one-time purchase that unlocks the full version of Crapette Pro is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. The App Store is contacted when you open the purchase screen, not before. Payment details and receipts are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy — we never see or store your payment information.

What we don't collect

Crapette Pro does not collect your name, email address, account credentials, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone access, browsing history, or any health or financial information. It contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting service — in fact, no third-party code at all.

Data retention and deletion

There is nothing to retain on our side, because nothing is collected. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the app or remove a specific saved game.

Children's privacy

Crapette Pro is intended for a general audience and is not directed at or designed for children under 13 — its strategic, multi-step gameplay is best suited to older players. Since the app collects nothing from anyone, it collects nothing from a child either, whatever their age. If you're a parent and have a question about this, contact us using the details below.

Your rights

Rights over personal data — access, correction, deletion, objection — apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none: there is nothing to look up, nothing to export and nothing to erase. If you're in the EU/EEA or California and have questions about how GDPR or CCPA applies here, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
